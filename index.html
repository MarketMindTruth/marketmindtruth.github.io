Who I Am

I'm a Gold trader. I trade $XAUUSD, nothing else. One instrument. One method. Every day.

I started trading during COVID in 2020. Like most people — beginner's luck, blown accounts, margin calls. The whole circus. But I didn't quit. I got obsessed. Not with making money — with understanding why I was losing it.

That obsession led me through every strategy on YouTube, every indicator combination, every "secret" method. And then, finally, to Auction Theory and Volume Profile. The moment I understood that price moves because of volume imbalances, not because of magic lines — everything clicked.

I'm anonymous. No face, no voice, no lifestyle flexing. I let the charts speak for themselves. Every morning I post a map with key levels. Later, I post proof of how price reacted. That's it.

I don't sell signals. I don't run paid groups. I don't promise you'll get rich. I just share what I see — and the market decides if I'm right.

Now I trade full-time. But let me be clear: I don't recommend this for beginners. When trading is your only income, every loss feels personal. If you're starting out, keep your job. Trade on the side. Build your process first.

My edge is patience. I wait for price to come to my levels. I don't chase. If the level reacts clean, I take the trade. If it doesn't, I move on. No ego. No revenge trades. Just process.

The market is the truth. I'm just the one reading it.
