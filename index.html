Who I Am

I'm a Gold trader. I trade $XAUUSD, nothing else. One instrument. One method. Every day.

I stumbled into trading a few years ago. No grand plan, no finance background. Just curiosity and way too much free time. What was supposed to be "let me try this thing" turned into an obsession pretty fast.

Of course, it started with beginner's luck. No rules, no planned entries, no risk management. Just clicking buttons and somehow making money. "Damn, I'm a natural!" That's what I thought. lol.

You already know how that ends. I blew my first account. Then parts of my second. The luck disappeared real fast when the market decided to actually move.

But I didn't quit. I got obsessed. Not with making money, but with understanding why I was losing it.

That obsession led me through everything. Every strategy on YouTube. Every indicator combination. Crypto, algos, "secret" methods. Nothing felt right. It all felt like guessing.

Then I found Auction Theory and Volume Profile. The moment I understood that price moves because of volume imbalances, not because of magic lines, everything clicked.

I'm anonymous. No face, no voice, no lifestyle flexing. I let the charts speak for themselves. Every morning I post a map with key levels. Later, I post proof of how price reacted. That's it.

I don't sell signals. I don't run paid groups. I don't promise you'll get rich. I just share what I see and the market decides if I'm right.

Now I trade full-time. But let me be clear: I don't recommend this for beginners. When trading is your only income, every loss feels personal. If you're starting out, keep your job. Trade on the side. Build your process first.

The market is the truth. I'm just the one reading it.
